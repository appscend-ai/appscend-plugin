name: today
description: "Work through today's list. Go through AppScend's ranked to-do list and do what can be done now."
argument-hint: "[app name or bundle ID]"
---

The app: $ARGUMENTS (when that's empty, the app in this repository; ask me which when AppScend has several).

Open AppScend's to-do list for that app (get_today) and work through it from the top. For each item: read its area with get_app, do what you can with the tools (each item's do is the call that acts on it: fixes on branches, syncs, test runs), and list what needs me (approvals, anything that reaches Apple or spends money, questions from my Mac). Finish with a short summary of what you did and what's left for me.
